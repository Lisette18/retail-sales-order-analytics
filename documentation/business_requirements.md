# Business Requirements

## Project Title

Retail Sales & Order Operations Analytics

## Business Problem

An e-commerce marketplace stores customer, order, product, payment,
and delivery information across separate operational datasets.
Management needs a consolidated analytical solution for monitoring
sales performance, understanding customer purchasing activity, and
identifying order and delivery problems.

## Project Objective

Develop an interactive Power BI dashboard that allows business users
to monitor sales, customers, products, payments, and delivery
performance.

## Intended Users

- Sales managers
- E-commerce operations managers
- Business analysts
- Data analysts
- Customer experience managers

## Business Questions

1. How are sales and order volume changing over time?
2. Which product categories generate the most revenue?
3. Which states contain the most customers and orders?
4. What payment methods do customers use?
5. How frequently do customers use payment installments?
6. What percentage of orders arrive after the estimated delivery date?
7. Which states and product categories experience the longest delivery times?
8. Which operational areas should management investigate?

## Key Performance Indicators

- Total Revenue
- Total Orders
- Unique Customers
- Average Order Value
- Average Freight Cost
- Average Delivery Time
- On-Time Delivery Rate
- Late-Delivery Rate
- Average Payment Value
- Average Payment Installments

## Project Scope

The first version will include:

- Sales and order analysis
- Customer geographic analysis
- Product-category analysis
- Payment-method analysis
- Delivery-performance analysis
- Data-quality validation

## Out of Scope for Initial Version

- Machine learning
- Sales forecasting
- Sentiment analysis
- Real-time data
- Customer review analysis
- Seller performance
